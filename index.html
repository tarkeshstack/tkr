My story

From writing mappings to leading delivery

I started in 2007 as a software engineer, writing Informatica mappings and Teradata queries. Three years on the ground in the US taught me to translate between business users and engineering teams, and that skill has shaped everything since.

I moved into project management and led 21+ projects, with teams of up to 25 people and stakeholders across India, the US, the UK and Europe. I have also led testing, as a Test Manager onsite in the UK for two years and a Quality Assurance Manager for five. Today I lead service delivery at TCS and champion Generative AI use cases for our customers.

Outside work I build small free web tools, record short lessons on my YouTube channel Life & Beyond, and coach people in communication, Informatica, spoken English and Hindi. The thread through all of it is the same: make it simple enough to use.
